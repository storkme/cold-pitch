How it's scored
  • Onset is the median pitch over the first 100 ms after your voice starts (change the window in settings). This is the number to train.
  • Settled is the median pitch from 450 ms onward, so it only appears if you hold the note for about a second.
  • Correction is how far you moved between the onset and the settled pitch. A scoop into the note shows up here.
  • Landed in is how long it took to stay within tolerance for 150 ms.
  • Any voiced sound during the hold voids the attempt, because humming to check is the habit this trains out.

Use headphones so the reference tone doesn't reach the mic. Start notes with a clean consonant like "da" or "ba" rather than easing in. On iPhone, headphones also stop the tone going quiet while the mic is open.
